THE SPIRIT OF TIHAR

More Than a Festival.

Tihar brings together devotion, nature, family and community. Across five days, Nepal celebrates meaningful relationships and the lights that brighten our homes.

FIVE DAYS OF CELEBRATION

Discover the Traditions

Explore the well-known celebrations of Tihar. Customs and observances can vary among communities.

DAY 01
🐦‍⬛

Kaag Tihar

Crows are honoured with offerings, traditionally associated with messages and communication.

DAY 02
🐕

Kukur Tihar

Dogs are celebrated for their loyalty, companionship and friendship with people.

DAY 03
🪔

Laxmi Puja

Homes are cleaned and illuminated with lamps while Goddess Laxmi is worshipped.

DAY 04
🐂

Govardhan Puja

Communities honour nature, agricultural life and animals, with traditions differing by region.

DAY 05
❤️

Bhai Tika

Siblings celebrate their bond with tika, blessings, gifts and time together.

🎶
🏘️

Deusi-Bhailo

Groups visit homes singing traditional festive songs and sharing good wishes.
